    What your first visit looks like

    Sixty minutes, one therapist, no hand-off to an aide. Here is how the hour is usually spent.

    1. 0–15 minYour storyWhen it started, what makes it better or worse, and what you want to get back to.
    2. 15–35 minMovement assessmentStrength, range of motion and the movements that bring on your pain, measured so we can track change.
    3. 35–50 minFirst treatmentHands-on care and, if it fits, a chiropractic adjustment or dry needling, with your consent.
    4. 50–60 minYour planTwo or three home exercises in our app, how often to come in, and what progress should look like.

    Questions

    Do I need a referral from my doctor?

    Minnesota allows direct access to physical therapy, so you may not need one to start. Some insurance plans still require a referral for coverage. We check your plan before your visit and tell you if one is needed.

    Physical therapy or chiropractic: which should I book?

    Book a physical therapy evaluation. Your therapist will bring in Dr. Abara if chiropractic care is likely to help, and you can see both in the same visit when it makes sense.

    How many visits will I need?

    It depends on the problem and your goals. After your evaluation we will give you an estimate and update it as you go. Many people come once or twice a week and do short home exercises in between.

    What should I wear?

    Clothes you can move in. Shorts for hip, knee and ankle problems, and a tank top or loose shirt for shoulder and neck problems.

    Do you offer dry needling?

    Yes, several of our therapists are trained in dry needling. It is always optional and only used with your consent.
