# Physical therapy clinic with a "where does it hurt?" body map

**Tier:** Medium · **Industry:** Physical therapy & chiropractic

An interactive front/back body map (inline SVG) lets patients tap the neck, shoulder, lower back, hip, knee or ankle to see the conditions commonly treated there and the suggested first visit, then book an evaluation: coverage (PPO, Medicare, workers' comp, auto, self-pay), day and time, details and confirmation. Also covers conditions, a minute-by-minute first visit, the care team, insurance and self-pay rates.

**Why it matters:** Patients describe pain by where it hurts, not by diagnosis. Letting them point to it, and telling them which visit to book and how they can pay, removes the two questions that usually stop people from booking online.

**Design:** Lexend throughout; deep blue, mist, teal, with coral used only for the body map highlight. Clean, data-like layout with motion on the body map (flip between views, pulsing selection). Single HTML file, no framework, keyboard accessible (hotspots work with Enter/Space and as buttons), reduced-motion aware, responsive down to 360 px.

The business, people, reviews and phone numbers (555 numbers) are fictional placeholders. Photos are free-licence images from Unsplash.
