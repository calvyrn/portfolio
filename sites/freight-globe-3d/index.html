    Questions shippers ask us

    How live is the live map?

    Vessel positions come from AIS, flights from the airline's status feed and trucks from the driver's ELD. We refresh every 15 minutes. In this concept the positions are simulated.

    Do you handle customs clearance?

    Yes. Our licensed customs brokers file your entry and ISF, arrange bonds and work out duty before the cargo lands, so you know the number in advance.

    What is the smallest shipment you take?

    One pallet by ocean LCL or 50 kg by air. Below that, a courier is usually cheaper and we'll tell you so.

    Can you hold containers if my warehouse is full?

    We pull containers off the terminal before demurrage starts and store them in our yard or transload into our bonded warehouse.

    Which ports do you cover besides Savannah?

    Charleston, Jacksonville and Brunswick for ocean, Atlanta and Charlotte for air. Savannah is home base.
