Questions

Is the coverage map accurate for my property?

The planner uses a sample property. On the site visit we model your actual building and lot the same way, including walls, trees and parked trucks.

Do I need a monthly plan?

No. You own the cameras and recorder. Remote monitoring is optional and month to month.

Can you reuse my existing cameras?

Often, yes. We test them first and only replace what can't record usable footage.

How long is footage kept?

Thirty days by default. We can size the recorder for 60 or 90 days if your insurer asks for it.
