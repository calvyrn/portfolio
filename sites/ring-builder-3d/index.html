From your design to your hand

Every ring is made to order by two goldsmiths at our bench above the shop. Most are ready in four to six weeks.

Step one

Pick the stone

We send three stones that match your design, with their grading reports and close-up video.

Step two

Approve the CAD

You get a detailed render of your ring at true size and can ask for changes until it's right.

Step three

Cast and set

We cast the setting in recycled metal, set the stone by hand and polish it under the microscope.

Step four

Collect or ship

Pick it up on King Street or we ship it insured, with a free resizing in the first year.

Questions

How close is the 3D ring to the real one?

Proportions, shape and metal colour are accurate. The exact sparkle depends on the stone you choose, which is why we send you video of real stones before we set one.

What if I don't know her ring size?

Pick your best guess. Resizing is free for the first year, and we can make the band so it resizes easily.

Do I pay anything up front?

A 30% deposit once you approve the stone and the CAD render. The rest is due when the ring is ready.

Is your gold and platinum recycled?

Yes, all our metal is recycled and refined in the US.
