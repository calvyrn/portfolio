Corn first. Everything else after.

We cook and grind our own masa from heirloom corn every morning, then press each tortilla when your order comes in. The salsas are made in small batches through the day, and the al pastor turns on the trompo from opening to close.

Masa y Lumbre started as a Saturday stand on East 6th. The window is still the heart of the place.

Why order here and not on a delivery app?

Delivery apps add their own fees and commission on every order. Ordering on our own site keeps prices the same as the counter.

You also get your order texted to you when it's ready, and your usual order saved for next time.
