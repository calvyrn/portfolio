Paying for it

Cash, loan or lease all work. We'll show you each side by side with your real numbers, and we never pressure you toward one.

Is it better to pay cash or finance?

Cash gives the biggest lifetime savings. A solar loan lets you start with little or nothing down, and in many cases the monthly payment is close to what you save on your bill. We'll show both on your quote.

How do solar loans work?

We work with several third-party lenders that offer fixed-rate loans from 10 to 25 years. You own the system from day one. Rates depend on your credit and the lender's current terms, and we show the APR and total cost before you sign anything.

What about leases and power purchase agreements?

With a lease or PPA, a third party owns the panels and you pay a monthly fee or a rate per kWh. There's no upfront cost, but your savings are smaller and selling the house needs a transfer. We'll tell you honestly when it makes sense.

Are there tax credits or rebates?

Incentives change often. We don't price in incentives you may not qualify for. Your quote shows the cost with no incentives, and we point you to a tax professional for anything that depends on your situation.

What if I sell my house?

Owned systems transfer with the house. Loans can usually be paid off at closing or, with some lenders, transferred. We send the buyer's agent the system paperwork and monitoring access.

Will solar work with my HOA?

Arizona law limits how far HOAs can block solar, but many still review panel placement. We prepare the drawings for your HOA and handle the back and forth.
