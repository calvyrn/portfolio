Process

Four days, start to finish, for a full coating.

  1. Inspect & measure

    We photograph every panel and read clear-coat depth with a gauge, so we know how far we can safely correct.

  2. Decontaminate

    Foam pre-wash, iron remover and clay to lift what washing leaves behind, including baked-on desert dust.

  3. Correct

    Machine polishing in stages until the paint is as flat and clear as it will safely go.

  4. Protect & cure

    Coating or film goes on in a dust-free bay, then 24 hours under infrared lamps before you drive it.

    Questions

    Before you drop off.

    Do I need paint correction before a coating?

    Almost always. A coating locks in whatever is under it, swirls included. Even new cars usually need a light one-stage polish from dealer prep.

    Can I wash my car right after a coating?

    Wait seven days. After that, a pH-neutral hand wash every two weeks keeps it performing. We include a care kit.

    Is PPF visible?

    Not from normal viewing distance. Edges are wrapped where the panel allows, and the film is optically clear with a gloss or matte finish.

    Do you offer a loaner or pickup?

    Enclosed pickup and delivery within 25 miles of the studio is free on jobs over $1,500.
