Before you order

How long does made-to-measure take?

Three weeks for festive pieces, ten to twelve weeks for bridal. If your event is sooner, call us; ready-to-wear sizes ship in two days.

How do I measure myself?

You need bust, waist and hip, plus your height. Each product has a short measuring guide, or come in and we'll do it in ten minutes.

Can I return a piece?

Ready-to-wear sizes can be returned unworn within 14 days. Made-to-measure pieces can't be returned, but alterations in the first 30 days are free.

Do you ship outside Texas?

Yes, anywhere in the US and Canada. Shipping is free over $250.

Can I reserve pieces to try on?

Add them to your bag and choose a time. We'll steam them and hold them in a fitting room for you, with no obligation to buy.
