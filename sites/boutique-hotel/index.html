The house on Jones Street

The Linden House was built as a family townhouse in the 1890s and spent most of the last century as apartments. The restoration kept what mattered: heart-pine floors, the curved stair, the garden wall and the linden tree that gives the house its name.

Today it is a small hotel run by a small team. You'll be greeted at the door, offered a glass of something cold and shown to your room, not handed a key card at a counter.

"We wanted it to feel like staying with friends who happen to have a very good bar."

Five walks from the front door

Savannah's historic district was laid out for walking. Ask at the front desk for our hand-drawn map.

  1. The big park with the fountain

    Thirty acres of live oaks and Spanish moss, a Saturday farmers market and the city's most photographed fountain.

    7 min walk

  2. The nearest square

    A quiet green square at the end of the block. Benches, shade and a good place for your morning coffee.

    2 min walk

  3. The riverfront

    Cobblestone ramps down to old cotton warehouses, ships passing on the river and evening strolls along the water.

    18 min walk

  4. The city market blocks

    Galleries, street musicians and a run of restaurants for dinner within a few blocks of each other.

    15 min walk

  5. The old cemetery

    Shaded paths, ironwork and centuries-old headstones. Calm and beautiful early in the morning.

    10 min walk

Before you arrive

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3 pm and check-out is at 11 am. Guests who book directly with us can stay until 1 pm on their last day. We're happy to hold bags if you arrive early or leave late.

Is there parking?

We offer valet parking for $28 a night, with in-and-out privileges. There is also a public garage two blocks away. Most guests find they don't need the car once they arrive.

Do you allow pets?

Dogs up to 40 lb are welcome in Courtyard King rooms for a $75 fee per stay. We'll have a bed, bowls and a map of the nearest squares waiting. Please mention your dog when you book.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. A cooked Southern breakfast is served from 7 to 10:30 am, in the garden when the weather allows.

Is the house accessible?

An elevator serves all three floors and one Courtyard King is fully accessible with a roll-in shower. Call us and we'll make sure it's held for you.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel without charge up to 72 hours before arrival. The exact terms for your dates are shown before you confirm.
