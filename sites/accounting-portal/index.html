    Questions we hear every January

    Something else? Call (919) 555-0112 or send a message from the portal.

    When should I send my documents?

    As soon as the last one arrives, usually mid-February. Returns with complete documents by March 15 are finished before the April rush. After that we'll still file on time, or file an extension and tell you why.

    Is the portal actually secure?

    In the real portal, files are encrypted in transit and at rest, logins use two-factor authentication, and staff access is logged. On this concept site, nothing leaves your browser at all.

    Can you take over books that are months behind?

    Yes. Catch-up work is quoted per month behind before we start. Most businesses are current within three to six weeks.

    Do you work with clients outside Raleigh?

    Yes. Most clients never visit the office. We prepare returns for any state where you have income and meet by video.

    What if I get a letter from the IRS?

    Upload it to the portal. If we prepared the return, responding to notices about it is included in your fee.

    Should my LLC become an S corporation?

    Sometimes. It depends on your profit, what a reasonable salary looks like in your field and the extra payroll cost. We run the numbers for a flat fee before you file anything.
