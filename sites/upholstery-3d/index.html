Velvet that gets better with use.

Questions

Before you order.

How long does a sofa take?

Six to eight weeks from the day you confirm fabric. We send a photo from the bench when the frame is done.

Will it fit up my stairs?

Send us your doorway and stairwell measurements. Legs come off, and the 98″ can be built in two sections that bolt together.

Can I use my own fabric?

Yes, customer's own material is welcome. You'll need about 14 yards for the 86″ sofa; we'll confirm after seeing a sample.

What if I don't like the colour in person?

That's what free swatches are for. Once a sofa is cut it's made for you, so we can't take it back, but we'll reupholster at cost within a year.
