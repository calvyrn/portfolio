    Questions, answered

    Still wondering about something? Call or text (404) 555-0186, 7 days a week.

    Do I need to provide supplies?

    No. Your team brings everything, including a vacuum and mop. If you prefer we use your own products, just leave them out with a note.

    What about pets?

    We love them. Tell us about your pets when you book. Our products are pet-safe, and we'll keep doors and gates closed as you ask.

    What if I'm not happy with the clean?

    Let us know within 24 hours and we'll send a team back to re-clean those areas for free. If it's still not right, we'll refund that visit.

    Do I need to be home?

    No. Most customers leave a key, a lockbox or a door code. Your access details are stored securely and only shared with your team.

    Can I reschedule or cancel?

    Yes, free of charge up to 48 hours before your visit. Later than that, there's a $40 fee because your team has already been scheduled.

    Should I tip?

    Never expected. If you'd like to, you can add a tip after the visit and 100% of it goes to your cleaners.
