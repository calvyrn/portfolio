Before you come

Are the 360° tours of the actual rooms?

Yes. Each tour is shot from the middle of the room, so what you see is the furniture, the window and the view you get. We update them whenever we change a room.

Is there a minimum stay?

Two nights on Fridays and Saturdays, three nights on holiday weekends. Weeknights can be booked one at a time.

Can we bring our dog?

Dogs are welcome in the Garden Room, which opens onto the lawn. There is a $35 cleaning fee per stay.

How far is the lake?

A four-minute walk down a gravel path to our dock, or a two-minute drive to the public beach at Lake Lure.

Do you have EV charging?

One Level 2 charger by the parking area, free for guests. Let us know when you book and we'll keep it open.
