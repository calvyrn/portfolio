Questions before you drop off

Do I need an appointment?

For oil changes and tire rotations we take walk-ins before 3 pm if a bay is open. For diagnosis and repairs, booking a drop-off means a technician is ready for your car when you arrive.

Will servicing my car here affect my factory warranty?

No. Federal law lets you have maintenance done at any qualified shop. We use parts that meet your manufacturer's specifications and record every service for you.

How does approving an estimate work?

After the inspection you get a text link with photos and an itemised estimate. Approve or decline each line. We only start the lines you approve, and the price you see is the price you pay.

What does the 24-month / 24,000-mile warranty cover?

Parts and labor on repairs we perform, for 24 months or 24,000 miles, whichever comes first. Bring the car back and we fix it at no charge.

Can I drop off my car after hours?

Yes. Use the key drop box by the side door, fill in the envelope and we'll text you when we start. The lot is lit and on camera.
