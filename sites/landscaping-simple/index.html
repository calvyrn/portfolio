Good to know

Quick answers to what we're asked most on walk-throughs.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The walk-through and written quote cost nothing. Paid design work only starts if you want a full planting plan, and that fee is credited back if we install it.

Do I need to be home for weekly mowing?

No. Just leave the gate unlocked and keep pets inside on your service day. We text when we arrive and when we leave.

When is the best time to plant in Charlotte?

Fall. October and November planting gives roots the winter to settle before summer heat. Spring works too, with a little more watering.

Do you handle HOA approvals?

We supply the drawing and plant list your HOA asks for, and we can adjust the plan if they request changes.
