    A tire shop, a drum roaster and a lot of Tuesdays.

    Ember Hill began on a tabletop roaster in a garage off Gallatin Pike. Today we roast on a restored 15-kilo drum in an old tire shop on Woodland Street, with the café in what used to be the waiting room.

    1. Sourced by the bagGreen coffee from importers who publish what farmers are paid.
    2. Roasted twice a weekTuesdays and Thursdays, in batches you can watch from the café.
    3. Rested, then groundBeans rest overnight; we grind to order the morning they ship.
    4. Shipped the same dayPacked in compostable bags with a one-way valve and the roast date.

    Good questions

    When is my coffee roasted?

    Subscription orders are roasted on the Tuesday or Thursday before they ship and leave the same afternoon. Most of the US receives them within 2 to 4 days of roasting.

    Which grind should I choose?

    Whole bean keeps longest if you have a grinder. Otherwise pick the grind for your brewer: espresso is fine, drip and pour-over medium, French press and cold brew coarse. You can change it before any delivery.

    How do I skip, pause or cancel?

    From your account, any time before Monday noon of a roast week. There are no fees or minimum number of deliveries.

    Do you have decaf?

    Yes, a Swiss Water Process decaf from Colombia, roasted medium. It's on the café shelf now and can be added to subscriptions by calling the roastery.
