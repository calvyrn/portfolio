How a Northstar trip comes together

Most trips take two to three weeks from first brief to final documents. You approve every step in writing.

  1. Step 01

    Send a four-minute brief

    Where, when, who's coming and what you love. The planner below takes about four minutes.

  2. Step 02

    A 30-minute call

    Your planner asks the questions that shape a trip: pace, hotels you've loved, how early you like to start.

  3. Step 03

    Your written proposal

    A day-by-day plan with hotels, guides, drivers and the full price, usually within five business days.

  4. Step 04

    Travel with a safety net

    Documents in one app, a local contact at every stop and our 24/7 line if a flight changes.

Questions before you start

Can't see yours? Call (720) 555-0148.

How much does a custom trip cost?

It depends on the route, season and hotels you choose. The sample prices above are per person for seven days, without international flights. Your full pricing, including our planning fee, is shown in your written proposal before you commit to anything.

Do you book flights from Denver?

Yes, if you'd like us to. We route from DEN with sensible connection times, or we can plan around flights you book with your own miles.

How far ahead should I start planning?

Four to six months is ideal, and nine to twelve for peak dates like cherry blossom in Kyoto or June on the Amalfi Coast. We can often do shorter notice outside peak season.

Can you plan honeymoons, family trips or group trips?

All three. Tell us about ages, mobility and the pace you like in the brief and we'll design around it.

What happens if something goes wrong while I'm away?

Call our 24/7 line. We handle missed connections, hotel issues and changes with our local partners so you don't have to.

Should I buy travel insurance?

We strongly recommend it for every international trip. We'll explain what to look for in a policy, and you choose the insurer.

Visit the studio

Coffee, maps on the table and a planner who has time for your questions. Video calls work just as well.
