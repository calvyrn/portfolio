Common questions

How much does the first consultation cost?

Nothing. The case review and the first conversation are free, with no obligation.

How long do I have to file a claim?

It depends on the type of case and can be as short as a few months for some employment claims. Send your enquiry early so a lawyer can check the deadline that applies to you.

What should I bring?

Anything you have: photos, medical records, letters from an insurer or employer, pay stubs. If you don't have them yet, we can help you request them.

Will my case go to court?

Most cases settle. If the offer isn't fair, we're prepared to go to trial and we'll tell you honestly what that involves.
