The studio02

We stay out of the way until it matters, then we're right there. Most of what you'll treasure happens between the moments you planned.

Juniper & Light is a husband-and-wife team working out of a small studio in the River Arts District. We take on about twenty weddings a year so every couple gets both of us, start to finish.

Expect very little posing. We'll guide family photos so they're done in fifteen minutes, then let the day unfold. Light, weather and whatever the mountains throw at us are part of the story.

We photograph mountaintop elopements, farm weddings in Madison County, downtown courthouse ceremonies, and travel to the coast a few times a year.

How booking works05

From hello to your gallery

Simple and unhurried. Most couples book 9 to 14 months ahead; for elopements, a few weeks can be enough.

  1. iSend an inquiryTell us your date and venue. We reply within two business days with availability and a quote.

  3. iiiReserve your dateSign the agreement online and pay the 30% retainer. Your date is held for you.

Questions and the studio08

Good questions

Do you travel outside Asheville?

Yes. Travel within 60 miles is included. Beyond that we add mileage, or flights and a room for destination weddings, quoted up front.

When will we see our photos?

A sneak peek of 30 or so images within a week, and your full gallery in six weeks. Elopement galleries arrive in three.

Do you give us the raw files?

No. Every image you receive is edited by us. You get full-resolution files and a print release, so you can print anywhere.

What if it rains?

We bring clear umbrellas and know covered spots at most local venues. Some of our favorite photographs were made in the rain.

Can we change the date after booking?

Your retainer moves with you to any open date within 18 months.
