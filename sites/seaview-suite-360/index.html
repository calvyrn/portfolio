About this place

The suite takes up the top of our house on the dune: a big bright bedroom, a bathroom with a soaking tub under the skylight, and a covered deck with a table for two looking straight out to sea.

It's quiet. There's no road between you and the beach, only a boardwalk through the bush. Bring a book, walk to the rock arch at low tide, and let the swell put you to sleep.

Questions

How far is the airport?

About 50 minutes by car from the nearest regional airport. We can book a transfer for you.

Is the sea safe to swim in?

The bay is sheltered at low tide. Lifeguards are on duty in December and January; we'll show you the safe spots.

Can you cook in the suite?

There's a fridge, kettle, coffee machine and microwave. For real cooking, use the shared braai and outdoor kitchen downstairs.

What's your cancellation policy?

Full refund up to 7 days before check-in, 50% after that.
