How we work

Four phases, each with a fixed fee and a clear deliverable. You can pause between any two of them.

  1. 01

    Site walk and brief

    We meet on the lot, measure what's there, check zoning and setbacks, and write down how you want to live in the house.

    2–3 weeksDeliverable: written brief and site study

  2. 02

    Schematic design

    Two or three plan options as models and sketches. We pick one together and refine it until it feels right.

    6–8 weeksDeliverable: plans, massing model, budget check

  3. 03

    Drawings and permits

    Construction drawings with our structural and MEP engineers, then submittal to the City of Austin or your county, and answers to every review comment.

    10–16 weeksDeliverable: permit set, specifications

  4. 04

    Building it

    We help you choose a builder, then visit the site every week, review every invoice and keep the details as drawn.

    Length of buildDeliverable: weekly site reports

Good questions to ask an architect

How much does an architect cost for a custom home?

For full service on a new house we usually land between 10% and 14% of the construction cost. We quote a fixed fee for each phase after the first meeting, so you know the number before any work starts.

How long until we can start building?

Most new homes take 7 to 10 months from the first site walk to a permit-ready set, depending on how fast decisions get made and how long the city or county review takes. Renovations and ADUs are usually shorter.

Do you handle the City of Austin permit?

Yes. We prepare and submit the residential permit set, track it through review, and answer every comment until it's approved. For lots outside the city we work with the county and any HOA design review.

Can we build an ADU on our lot?

Often yes, but it depends on lot size, zoning, setbacks, trees and utilities. Our first step for any ADU is a short feasibility check so you know what's allowed before paying for design.

Do you work with our builder?

Happily. If you already have a builder we bring them in early for pricing. If not, we'll introduce two or three we trust and help you compare bids line by line.
