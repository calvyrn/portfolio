    Questions we hear every summer

    Do you really answer at 2 am?

    Yes. Calls after hours go to an on-call dispatcher in Tampa, not an answering service. If it's an emergency, a technician is sent the same night.

    How much is an after-hours visit?

    Our service call is $89 and is waived when you approve the repair. Visits in the 4–8 pm window today, overnight, Sundays and holidays add a $79 after-hours fee. Comfort Club members never pay it.

    My AC is running but blowing warm air. Is it an emergency?

    Turn the system off to protect the compressor, check the breaker and the filter, then book a window. If anyone in the home is elderly, an infant or has a medical condition, tell the dispatcher and we'll prioritize your call.

    Should I repair or replace my system?

    If your system is over 12–15 years old and the repair is a large share of a new unit's cost, replacing often makes sense. Your technician will give you both prices in writing, with no pressure either way.

    Do you work on all brands?

    Yes, all major brands of central air conditioners, heat pumps, air handlers and ductless systems.
