Questions, answered

Still unsure? Call the studio at (619) 555-0143, Tuesday to Saturday.

How fast does shipping take?

Orders placed before noon Pacific ship the same day from San Diego; everything else ships within two business days. Standard US delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and is free over $60, otherwise $6. Express (1 to 2 days) is $14. We ship to all 50 states but not internationally yet.

Can I return a serum I've opened?

Yes. If a serum isn't right for you, send it back within 30 days of delivery, opened or not, and we refund the full price to your original payment method. We email you a prepaid label; there is no restocking fee.

I have sensitive skin. Where should I start?

Start with Hyaluronic Water, which is fragrance-free and has the shortest ingredient list. Patch test on your inner arm for 48 hours before using anything new on your face. Introduce one serum at a time, two weeks apart. If you have a diagnosed skin condition, ask your dermatologist first.

Can I use vitamin C and retinol together?

We suggest splitting them: vitamin C in the morning under sunscreen, retinol at night. Hyaluronic Water layers with both. If you're pregnant or breastfeeding, skip retinol and check with your doctor.

How long does a bottle last?

At three to four drops a day, a 30 ml bottle lasts about two months and a 15 ml about one. Each label shows a best-before month; vitamin C is freshest within 4 months of opening.
