New to Willow Creek?

Your first visit takes about 45 minutes. Here's how to make it easy on you and your pet.

  1. Book online or callChoose "First visit" and we'll allow extra time for introductions and history.
  2. Send past recordsGive us your old clinic's name and we'll request vaccine and medical records for you.
  3. Fill in a short formA link arrives by email. It takes five minutes and saves time at the front desk.
  4. Come in a little hungryWe use lots of treats. If your pet might need bloodwork, skip breakfast that morning.

What to bring

  • Cats in a carrier, dogs on a leash (we have spare leashes)
  • Any medications or supplements, or a photo of the labels
  • A fresh stool sample for puppies, kittens and sick visits
  • A list of questions; there are no silly ones

Questions pet owners ask us

Can't find yours? Call (208) 555-0127. We're happy to talk it through.

Do you take walk-ins?

We hold same-day sick-visit slots every weekday morning, so call or book online first and we'll give you the soonest time. For true emergencies, call us right away.

What should I do after hours?

If we're closed and your pet needs urgent care, go to the nearest 24-hour emergency veterinary hospital. Our phone message lists the closest ones. We'll get their records the next morning.

Do you accept pet insurance?

Yes. Most pet insurance reimburses you directly, and we'll fill in claim forms and send records at no charge.

Do you see rabbits and other small pets?

Yes: rabbits, guinea pigs, ferrets and rats. Book "Other" online and tell us the species. We don't treat birds or reptiles.

How much is a dental cleaning?

It depends on your pet's age, size and what the X-rays show. You'll get a written estimate with a low and high range before the day, and we call you before doing anything outside it.

Can I stay with my pet during the exam?

Almost always. Most exams and vaccines happen with you in the room. For X-rays or blood draws we may step into the treatment area for a few minutes.
