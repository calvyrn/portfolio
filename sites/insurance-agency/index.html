Common questions

Does it cost more to buy through an independent agent?

No. Insurance companies pay agencies a commission that is built into the premium whether you buy through an agent or not. You do not pay us a fee to compare quotes.

Does my homeowners policy cover flooding?

Standard homeowners policies do not cover flood damage. Flood insurance is a separate policy, and there is usually a 30-day waiting period before federal flood coverage starts, so it is worth asking early.

What is a wind or hurricane deductible?

Near the coast, many policies have a separate deductible for wind and hail, often a percentage of your home's insured value. We show it next to the price in every comparison so you can see the real difference.

Can I keep my current policy until renewal?

Yes. Many clients ask us to quote 30 to 45 days before renewal. If you switch mid-term, most carriers refund the unused premium.

Which insurance companies do you work with?

We are appointed with several national and regional carriers. Which ones can quote depends on the coverage and your property, and we will tell you exactly who quoted.
