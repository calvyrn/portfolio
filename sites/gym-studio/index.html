    Questions

    Anything else, call or text (614) 555-0165. A coach answers, not a call center.

    I'm out of shape. Is this too intense?

    No. Every workout has a scaled version, and most members started with no lifting experience. Your coach picks weights with you in the first few classes.

    What should I bring to my first class?

    Water, flat shoes (running shoes are fine for day one) and clothes you can move in. Chalk, belts and towels are here.

    How does the waitlist work?

    If someone cancels, the first person on the waitlist gets the spot and a text. Cancel at least two hours before class so the next person has time to come.

    Can I freeze my membership?

    Yes, up to two months a year for travel, injury or anything else. Just tell the front desk or use the app.

    Is there parking?

    Free lot in front of the building with 40 spaces, plus street parking on W 3rd Ave.

    Do you have showers and lockers?

    Two private showers, day lockers with your own lock, and a changing room. Towels are $1 or free on Unlimited.
