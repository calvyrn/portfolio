New patients are usually seen the same week. Here is what to expect.

  1. Book online or callChoose a time that suits you. You'll get a confirmation text right away.
  2. Fill in forms on your phoneA secure link arrives the day before, so there's no clipboard in the waiting room.
  3. Exam, X-rays and cleaningAbout 60 to 75 minutes with the hygienist and the dentist.

Common questions

Do you see emergencies the same day?

Yes. We keep openings every weekday morning for pain, swelling and broken teeth. Book "Tooth pain" online or call and we'll fit you in.

How much is a visit without insurance?

You'll get the exact price before treatment. Our membership plan covers two cleanings, exams and X-rays each year for one flat yearly fee.

Can I reschedule online?

Yes. Every confirmation text has a link to move or cancel your appointment up to 24 hours before.

Do you treat children?

Yes, from age 3. Book the whole family back to back and we'll see you in one trip.
