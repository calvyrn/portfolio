Questions, answered

Anything else, call or text (480) 555-0176 and the front desk will help.

Is the consultation really complimentary?

Yes. Consultations are free and take about 30 minutes. If you decide to go ahead the same day and your provider agrees it is suitable, we can usually treat you in the same visit.

Who performs injectables?

Injectables are performed only by registered nurse injectors, working under the supervision of our licensed medical director. Facials, peels and dermaplaning are performed by licensed aestheticians.

Will I see results after one session?

It depends on the treatment and on you. Some treatments need a series of sessions, and results vary from person to person. Your provider will tell you what is realistic before you start.

Is there downtime?

Many treatments have little or none, while others may cause redness or swelling for a few days. You will get written aftercare instructions and a direct number to call with questions.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us 24 hours' notice to move or cancel. Late cancellations for treatments over $300 may carry a $50 fee; consultations never do.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, monthly payment plans are available for treatment plans over $500, subject to approval. Ask at your consultation.
